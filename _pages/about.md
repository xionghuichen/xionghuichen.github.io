**Research Interests:**  
My research focuses on reinforcement learning (RL) and foundation models for real-world decision-making. My recent work spans embodied foundation models, scalable RL for language and vision-language models, and multimodal reasoning and generation. These directions build on my earlier research in sim-to-real transfer, offline RL, causal inference, and environment modeling, with applications in robotics, autonomous driving, recommender systems, and industrial control.

#### Recent Research (2025–2026)

- **Embodied foundation models: Qwen-Robot.** The Qwen-Robot series develops foundation models for robotic manipulation ([Qwen-RobotManip](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17846)), navigation ([Qwen-RobotNav](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18112)), and language-conditioned world modeling ([Qwen-RobotWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17030)).
- **RL for foundation models.** During the development of Qwen3 through Qwen4, I served as a core contributor to Qwen's RL and a principal contributor to the foundation model team's internal RL algorithms, RL loop, and RL climbing pipeline for training large-scale foundation models, as well as the initial design of Qwen's asynchronous RL infrastructure. My contributions during this period spanned chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning, vision-language (VL) models, agentic systems, and coding. Related work includes [GSPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.18071) for stable and efficient policy optimization, [Beyond the 80/20 Rule](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/a797c2d2e0c1fdabf4d1ab8cd0b465c6-Abstract-Conference.html) on high-entropy tokens in RL for reasoning, and [ReVal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23355) for off-policy, value-based learning with trajectory reuse.
- **Multimodal reasoning and generation.** [Qwen3-VL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21631) develops long-context multimodal understanding and reasoning, and [Qwen-Image-2.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10730) unifies image generation and editing.
